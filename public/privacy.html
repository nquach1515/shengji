Privacy Policy

Shengji (Tractor · 升级), the iOS app and shengji-tractor.com · Effective November 8, 2026

Shengji is a card game. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect, sell or share personal data.

What stays on your device

  • The name you type, your game settings and display preferences.
  • A saved copy of a table you're hosting, so you can pick it back up.

These are kept in the app's (or your browser's) local storage and never leave your device except as described below. Deleting the app or clearing the site's data removes them.

Playing online with friends

When you host or join a table, your chosen name and your moves are sent to the other players at that table through our relay server, so the game can run. The relay only forwards these messages while the table is open; it doesn't store them, and nothing about the game is kept after everyone leaves. Bot games run entirely on your device.

App updates

The app checks shengji-tractor.com for a newer version of the game when it opens and downloads it if there is one. This request contains no personal information.

Hosting

The website and relay run on Cloudflare, which, like any web host, processes standard connection information (such as IP addresses) to deliver the service. We don't use it to identify or track you.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: support@shengji-tractor.com.
