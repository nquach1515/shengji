How do I play with friends?

On the home screen choose Host a table, then share the invite link or the four-letter code. Friends can join from the app or from shengji-tractor.com in any browser. Empty seats can be filled with bots.

How do I learn the rules?

Tap How to play on the home screen or in the game menu. The Hint button suggests a move on your turn.

The sound is off

Check the speaker button at the top of the table, and the silent switch on your iPhone — game sounds follow it.
